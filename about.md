I use this site to document practical engineering lessons from cloud, infrastructure, monitoring, cost, security, and AI workload reviews.

Most posts come from the same starting point: understand the environment before recommending changes. That means looking at data flows, access paths, logging, backup posture, cost patterns, security controls, operational ownership, and the tradeoffs clients have to make when technical debt meets real deadlines.

The [resume page]({{ "/resume/" | relative_url }}) keeps the same focus and turns the project work into a portfolio view.

The writing here is meant to be direct and useful. I care most about the questions, decisions, and lessons that tend to matter when the work has to hold up in production.
